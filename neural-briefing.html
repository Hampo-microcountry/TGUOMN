Welcome to TGUOMN Audio Briefings. TGUOMN stands for The Great Union of Micro Nations. It is an alliance created to encourage cooperation, diplomatic communication, mutual respect, and shared learning among micronations. Each member can keep its own identity, leadership, and internal rules while working with other members on common goals. The Union website publishes information about its structure, charter, member directory, laws, announcements, and history. A micronational organization is not the same thing as internationally recognized sovereign states. Thanks for listening to this introductory TGUOMN audio briefing.
