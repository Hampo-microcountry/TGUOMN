Union Charter

This page is reserved for the official charter of TGUOMN.

Draft Foundational Principles

  1. Members shall communicate through peaceful and diplomatic means.
  2. Members retain domestic independence, subject to obligations they accept under adopted Union rules.
  3. Union laws and decisions should be published with their current status.
  4. Membership and administrative procedures should be publicly documented.
  5. Amendments to the charter should be recorded and published.

Draft These principles are a draft. Replace this text with the final charter once formally approved.
