Law 002 — Member Sovereignty and Domestic Independence

Proposed

Status: Proposed, not enacted.

Scheduled consideration: 16 October 2026.

Purpose

This proposed law affirms the domestic independence of participating micronations.

Proposed provisions

  1. Each member retains its own domestic leadership, institutions, and internal decision-making.
  2. Union participation does not by itself replace a member's domestic governing authority.
  3. Union-wide cooperation should respect members' domestic independence, alongside any Union rules formally adopted.

This text is a proposal and must not be treated as adopted until formally passed and published.
