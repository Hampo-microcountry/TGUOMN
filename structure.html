Organization and Administration

Founder

The founder establishes the Union and exercises the authority provided by its adopted founding rules, including decisions on membership acceptance and removal.

Member Representatives

Each member may designate an authorized representative to communicate with the Union and submit official requests.

Administrative Records

Membership decisions, legislation, announcements, and opt-out notices should be recorded consistently.

Administration and Accountability

Official decisions should be communicated clearly and distinguished from proposals or informal discussions.
