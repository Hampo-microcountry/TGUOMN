Five founding laws are scheduled for consideration on 16 October 2026. Until formally passed, they remain proposed rather than enacted.

  • Law 001 — Founder's Authority and Leadership
  • Law 002 — Member Sovereignty and Domestic Independence
  • Law 003 — Domestic Opt-Out Rights
  • Law 004 — Continued Validity of Alliance Laws
  • Law 005 — Diplomatic Communication and Administration

Proposed Scheduled consideration: 16 October 2026

View All Laws

Domestic Independence and Opt-Out Rights

A member's authorized governing authority may request an opt-out from domestic enforcement of an alliance law. Such an opt-out applies to that member's domestic enforcement only; it does not invalidate the law for other members or automatically end membership in the Union.
