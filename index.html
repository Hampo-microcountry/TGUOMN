  1. Founder's Authority and Leadership
  2. Member Sovereignty and Domestic Independence
  3. Domestic Opt-Out Rights
  4. Continued Validity of Alliance Laws
  5. Diplomatic Communication and Administration

Member Sovereignty and Domestic Independence

Under the proposed framework, a member's authorized governing authority may request an exemption from the domestic enforcement of a Union law. Such an exemption would apply to that member rather than automatically invalidating the law for other members.
