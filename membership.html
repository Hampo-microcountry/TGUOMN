Membership and Admission

This page describes the intended membership process. Confirm the final rules with the Union's adopted procedures.

Application Process

  1. Provide the micronation's name and a brief description.
  2. Identify an authorized representative.
  3. Provide an official contact method.
  4. Wait for an official acceptance notice.

Membership Decisions

Acceptance and removal decisions should be communicated by the authorized Union administrator.

For inquiries, visit Contact the Union.
