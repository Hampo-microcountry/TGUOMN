Domestic Opt-Out Register

This page is intended to record opt-out notices submitted under the Union's adopted procedures.

No opt-out notices have been published.

Public Record Format

  • Member micronation.
  • Law concerned.
  • Date the request was received.
  • Effective date, if confirmed.
  • Reference to the applicable procedure.

A member's president, parliament, or other authorized governing authority may submit an opt-out request to the founder for a particular law. The request affects domestic enforcement for that member only; it does not invalidate the law for other members or automatically end membership.

Submit requests through the official channel listed on the Contact page. Do not publish confidential correspondence or private contact details.
