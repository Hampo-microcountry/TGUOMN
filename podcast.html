What is TGUOMN?

Audio briefing Read aloud by your device

Choose a voice style and press Play. Available voice quality depends on your phone or browser.

The player selects four different voices available on your device when at least four are installed. Voice realism depends on your device’s speech engine; this player does not generate studio-quality human recordings.

Make TGUOMN one tap away

Install TGUOMN on your phone's home screen to open the Union's pages and audio briefings like a standalone app.

Android: use “Add to Home” if your browser offers it, or open the browser menu and choose “Install app” / “Add to Home screen.”

iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then choose “Add to Home Screen.”
