Law 003 — Domestic Opt-Out Rights

Proposed

Status: Proposed, not enacted.

Scheduled consideration: 16 October 2026.

Purpose

This proposed law establishes a process for members to opt out of domestic enforcement of an individual Union law.

Proposed provisions

  1. A member's president, parliament, or other authorized governing authority may submit an opt-out request to the founder for any individual Union law.
  2. A request may be submitted at any time through the Union's designated official communication channel.
  3. An opt-out affects domestic enforcement of that law for the requesting member only.
  4. An opt-out does not invalidate the law for other members or automatically terminate the requesting member's Union membership.

This text is a proposal and must not be treated as adopted until formally passed and published.
