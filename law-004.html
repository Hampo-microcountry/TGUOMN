Law 004 — Continued Validity of Alliance Laws

Proposed

Status: Proposed, not enacted.

Scheduled consideration: 16 October 2026.

Purpose

This proposed law clarifies that an individual member's domestic opt-out does not cancel a Union law for other members.

Proposed provisions

  1. A Union law, once formally adopted, remains part of the Union's legislative record unless it is amended, repealed, or otherwise changed through an authorized Union process.
  2. A member's opt-out affects only domestic enforcement within that member's jurisdiction.
  3. Opting out does not itself remove the law's validity for other members.
  4. The public legislative register should clearly distinguish proposed, adopted, amended, and repealed laws.

This text is a proposal and must not be treated as adopted until formally passed and published.
