Law 001 — Founder's Authority and Leadership

Proposed

Status: Proposed, not enacted.

Scheduled consideration: 16 October 2026.

Purpose

This proposed law describes the founder's administrative role within The Great Union of Micro Nations.

Proposed provisions

  1. The founder serves as the Union's principal administrator, subject to the Union's formally adopted founding rules.
  2. The founder controls decisions to accept new members and remove existing members.
  3. Official membership decisions should be communicated clearly and recorded in the Union's public administrative records.

This text is a proposal and must not be treated as adopted until formally passed and published.
