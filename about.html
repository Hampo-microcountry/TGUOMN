About the Union

The Great Union of Micro Nations (TGUOMN) is a micronational alliance established to encourage cooperation, diplomatic communication, and shared administration among participating micronations.

Our Purpose

The Union provides a framework for collaboration while respecting the domestic independence of its members.

Our Principles

  • Cooperation and peaceful relations.
  • Respect for member sovereignty.
  • Transparent publication of laws and decisions.
  • Diplomatic communication between members.

TGUOMN is a micronational organization and does not automatically possess international recognition as a sovereign state.
