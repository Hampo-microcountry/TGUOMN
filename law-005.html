Law 005 — Diplomatic Communication and Administration

Proposed

Status: Proposed, not enacted.

Scheduled consideration: 16 October 2026.

Purpose

This proposed law sets expectations for clear official communication and consistent administrative records within TGUOMN.

Proposed provisions

  1. Official communications should identify the sender, the member or office represented, and the subject of the communication.
  2. Membership decisions, legislative status changes, announcements, and opt-out notices should be recorded accurately.
  3. Public records should distinguish proposals from formally adopted decisions.
  4. Claims about external recognition or diplomatic agreements should be published only when confirmed.

This text is a proposal and must not be treated as adopted until formally passed and published.
